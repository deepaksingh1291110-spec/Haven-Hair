How it Works

Effortless for everyone.

The entire barbershop experience — redesigned around your time.

01

Find your barber

See nearby shops on the map with live queue status, wait times, and ratings — all in real time.

02

Grab your token

Book a slot or grab a walk-in token. Track your position from home — no plastic chair required.

03

Arrive on time

Get notified when you're 2 customers away. Walk in, sit down, get your cut. Simple.

04

Leave your review

Your verified review shapes the reputation of the shop — keeping quality high for everyone.

Features

Built for both sides.

🗺️

Live Map

See every open barbershop near you with real-time queue counts and wait times on an interactive map.

🪙

Virtual Token

Grab a walk-in token without going to the shop. Track your queue position from anywhere.

🔔

Smart Alerts

Get notified when you're 2 customers away. No more guessing, no more wasted time.

📅

Advance Booking

Reserve your slot in advance. Pick your barber, pick your time, show up ready.

⭐

Verified Reviews

Only real customers who booked through Haven Hair can leave reviews — no fake ratings.

🔍

Smart Filters

Filter by distance, rating, availability, or specialty. Find exactly what you need fast.

👆

2-Button System

Your entire workflow is two taps. Customer Sits → Haircut Done. Nothing more, nothing less.

🔁

Auto Queue

Queue manages itself. Tokens issue automatically, wait times update instantly for everyone.

🛡️

No-Show Protection

If a booked slot passes without being started, the system auto-skips and moves the queue forward.

💬

Customer Outreach

The system flags unhappy customers and nudges you to reach out — in your own words, always.

📊

Weekly Reports

Every week, a full summary — complaints, happy customers, reputation change, and insights.

🏅

Reputation Score

Your shop gets scored and awarded badges. Top Rated shops appear first in customer search.
